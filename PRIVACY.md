# Lift Relay Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Lift Relay is designed as an offline-first workout companion. Workout plans, exercise history, gym profiles, preferences, and the app's local analytics buffer are stored on your device. Lift Relay does not operate a user-account service and does not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising.

## Information handled by the app

- **Workout and fitness information:** exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, session history, equipment availability, and gym-profile settings that you enter. This information remains on your device.
- **Voice input:** microphone audio is processed through Apple's Speech framework only when you choose voice logging. Apple may process speech according to your device settings and Apple's privacy terms. Lift Relay does not store or transmit the audio to a Lift Relay server.
- **Purchase information:** subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Lift Relay receives only the entitlement and transaction information needed to unlock Pro features; payment details are handled by Apple.
- **Diagnostics:** the current release keeps a privacy-conscious analytics buffer locally on the device. It is not sent to Lift Relay or an advertising network.

## Data sharing and retention

Lift Relay does not share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data stored by the app remains until you delete it in the app, remove the app, or erase the device. Subscription records are retained by Apple under Apple's policies.

## Children

Lift Relay is a general fitness tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Health notice

Lift Relay provides general fitness-planning tools and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before beginning or changing an exercise programme when appropriate.

## Your choices

You can export your workout data or delete workout history from Lift Relay Settings. You can deny or revoke microphone and speech-recognition access in iOS Settings. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data, subject to device backups managed by you or Apple.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public [Lift Relay support repository](https://github.com/lanray07/Lift-Relay/issues).

This policy may be updated when Lift Relay's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
