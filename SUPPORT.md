# Lift Relay Support

For help with Lift Relay, check the steps below or [open a support issue](https://github.com/lanray07/Lift-Relay/issues/new).

## Purchases and subscriptions

- In Lift Relay, open Settings and choose **Restore Purchases** if a Pro entitlement is missing.
- Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS under **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions**.
- Apple handles billing and refunds. Use [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) for purchase support.

## Voice logging

Voice logging requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. Review access under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and **Speech Recognition**.

## Data and privacy

Lift Relay is offline-first. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data, subject to device backups managed by you or Apple. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

When opening an issue, include the iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, app version, and a concise description of what happened. Do not post sensitive health or payment information.
